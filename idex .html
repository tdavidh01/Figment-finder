Before you order wheels

Check your bolt pattern

Make sure the wheel's bolt pattern matches your vehicle. Never assume a wheel fits based on diameter alone.

Confirm hub bore & hardware

Check center bore, lug bolt or nut seat type, thread size, and safe engagement. Spacers may require specific longer hardware.

Check real-world clearance

Suspension, brakes, tire brand, ride height, alignment, and fender shape affect fit. Test fit before driving.

Wheel fitment FAQ

Does this calculator guarantee a wheel will fit?

No. It compares dimensions mathematically, but cannot verify brake clearance, suspension clearance, fender clearance, load rating, or vehicle-specific fitment. Always confirm with a qualified wheel/tire professional.

What does wheel offset mean?

Offset (ET) is the distance in millimeters between the wheel's mounting face and its centerline. A higher positive offset generally moves the wheel farther inward; a lower offset generally moves it outward.

Will a wider tire always stick out farther?

Not always. Wheel width, offset, tire model, and the rim's measured width all affect the final position. This tool provides a simplified estimate.
